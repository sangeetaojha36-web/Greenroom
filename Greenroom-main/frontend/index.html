    Camera Access Required

    Interview cannot start without webcam permission

    GreenRoom operates as a realistic, proctored mock interview room. In order to begin your rehearsal session, an active camera feed is required by the system.

    To proceed with your interview:

    • Ensure your webcam is connected and not in use by another app.
    • Click Allow on your browser's camera permission prompt.
    • If blocked previously, click the lock/camera icon in your browser URL bar and reset permissions.
